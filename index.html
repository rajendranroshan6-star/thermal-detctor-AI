Thermal cameras have always shown us where heat is. The gap has been telling a system what that heat means — is that bright patch a person, a fire risk, or a bearing about to seize? THERMAL/AI closes that gap with a lightweight model trained to classify heat signatures on-device, no cloud round-trip required.

How a frame becomes an alert

Four stages, running continuously, from raw sensor to decision.

CAPTURE

Raw thermal frame

A microbolometer array reads a 32×24 – 640×480 grid of temperature values, 9–60 times per second.

NORMALIZE

Calibrate & denoise

Ambient drift is corrected, dead pixels are interpolated, and the frame is scaled into the model's expected range.

INFER

Model reads the heat

A compact CNN segments hotspots and classifies each region: human, machine, ambient, anomaly.

ACT

Threshold & alert

Confirmed anomalies cross a configurable threshold and trigger a webhook, log entry, or on-site alarm.

Built for the field, not just the demo

Edge-first inference

Runs on Raspberry Pi–class hardware. No connectivity required for detection to function.

Multi-zone tracking

Define independent detection zones per frame, each with its own thresholds and alert rules.

False-positive filtering

Temporal smoothing across frames rejects one-off sensor noise and sun glare before an alert fires.

Open alert hooks

Plain webhook and MQTT output — wire it into whatever dashboard or siren you already run.

Where a temperature difference matters

Industrial

Spot overheating motors, bearings, and electrical panels before they fail or ignite.

Perimeter

Detect human presence in low-light or zero-light conditions along a fence line or site boundary.

Wildfire

Flag small, hot, fast-spreading heat sources against a cooler forest canopy background.

Screening

Surface elevated skin temperature at entry points as a first-pass, non-contact check.
